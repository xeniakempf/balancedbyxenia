A nurse practitioner–patient relationship begins when you complete your initial appointment and we agree to provide care. Virtual care

Consultations take place at our Austin office or by secure video or phone. Virtual care has limits: we cannot do a physical examination, and technical problems can interrupt a visit. If we decide that you need an in-person assessment, we will arrange one at our Austin office or refer you locally.

Please join from a private place with a reliable connection. You are responsible for the privacy of your own device and surroundings.

  • Some medications, including testosterone, are controlled substances and are prescribed only in line with federal and Texas law, including DEA requirements and Texas Board of Nursing rules.
  • Medications are filled by the pharmacy you choose. Lab tests are performed by independent laboratories. We are not responsible for their services, pricing or availability.
